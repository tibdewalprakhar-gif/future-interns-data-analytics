# Future Interns – Data Science & Analytics Internship

A collection of three practical data analytics projects completed as part of the Future Interns Data Science & Analytics Internship.

This repository demonstrates the use of Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, and Power BI to analyze datasets, identify patterns, create visualizations, and communicate data-driven insights.

## Projects Overview

### 1. Business Sales Performance Analytics

<text>**Folder:** `FUTURE_DS_01/`</text>

**Objective:** Analyze business sales data to understand sales performance, profitability, regional trends, and product category performance.

**Key Highlights:**
- Analyzed 9,994 sales records.
- Calculated total sales, total profit, orders, and profit margin.
- Compared sales and profitability across categories and regions.
- Identified loss-making product subcategories.
- Created an interactive Power BI dashboard.

**Key Results:**

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Sales | $2,297,200.86 |
| Total Profit | $286,397.02 |
| Total Orders | 5,009 |
| Profit Margin | 12.47% |

**Tools:** Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Power BI.

[View Project 1](./FUTURE_DS_01/)

---

### 2. Customer Retention & Churn Analysis

**Folder:** `FUTURE_DS_02/`

**Objective:** Analyze customer churn patterns and identify customer segments associated with higher churn rates.

**Key Highlights:**
- Analyzed 7,043 customer records.
- Calculated overall customer churn and retention.
- Compared churn rates across contract types.
- Examined churn patterns by internet service and customer tenure.
- Created visual reports and a Power BI dashboard.

**Key Results:**

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Customers | 7,043 |
| Retained Customers | 5,174 |
| Churned Customers | 1,869 |
| Churn Rate | 26.54% |

**Tools:** Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Power BI.

[View Project 2](./FUTURE_DS_02/)

---

### 3. Marketing Campaign Performance Analysis

**Folder:** `FUTURE_DS_03/`

**Objective:** Analyze bank marketing campaign outcomes and understand customer subscription patterns across different job categories and contact methods.

**Key Highlights:**
- Analyzed 45,211 campaign records.
- Calculated the overall term-deposit subscription rate.
- Compared subscription rates across job categories.
- Evaluated campaign outcomes by contact method.
- Created charts, summary reports, and a Power BI dashboard.

**Key Results:**

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Campaign Records | 45,211 |
| Successful Subscriptions | 5,289 |
| Unsuccessful Outcomes | 39,922 |
| Subscription Rate | 11.70% |

**Tools:** Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Power BI.

[View Project 3](./FUTURE_DS_03/)

**Dataset Note:** This project uses bank marketing campaign data. It analyzes campaign contacts and subscription outcomes, not a complete website visitor-to-customer conversion funnel.
